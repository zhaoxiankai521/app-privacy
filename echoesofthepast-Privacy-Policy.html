Privacy Policy

Echoes of the Past (the "App")

Last updated: September 9, 2026 · Effective date: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Zhao Xiankai (赵宪开) ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile application Echoes of the Past. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. No Data Collection

The App is designed to run entirely on your device. It:

  • Does not connect to the internet at any time during use;
  • Does not require an account or registration;
  • Does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data or usage information;
  • Contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Because the App does not collect any personal information, there is nothing about you that we receive, process, or share with anyone.

3. Local-Only Storage

Your game progress — including save slots, settings, and language preference — is stored locally on your device using the device's own local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to any server.

4. In-App Purchases (if introduced in the future)

If we add optional in-app purchases in a future update, those transactions are processed by Apple and governed by Apple's own terms. We do not receive or store your payment information. Any such feature would be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children. Because no personal data is collected at all, there is nothing to collect from any user, regardless of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our practices change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" above.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: zhaoxiankai521@foxmail.com

8. Your Rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete. You may delete all local App data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.
