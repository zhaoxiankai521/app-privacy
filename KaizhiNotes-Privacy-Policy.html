Kaizhi Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026　|　Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Who We Are

This application, "Kaizhi Notes" (开智笔记), is developed and maintained by an independent developer (referred to as "we"). If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at zhaoxiankai521@foxmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any of your personal data or note content. Kaizhi Notes is a local-first note and knowledge management tool. All of your notes, mind maps, outlines, tasks, and favorites are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server controlled or operated by us.

3. Device Permissions

Kaizhi Notes requests only a small number of device permissions, solely to enable features you choose to use, and never to collect data:

  • Camera — to take photos and insert them into your notes (NSCameraUsageDescription). Captured photos are stored inside your notes on your device or in your own iCloud; they are never uploaded to us.
  • Microphone — to record voice notes (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription). Audio is stored locally or in your own iCloud; it is never uploaded to us.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to unlock the app when you enable the device lock (NSFaceIDUsageDescription). Biometric authentication is handled entirely by the operating system; biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessed by us.

The app does not request access to your system Photo Library; captured images remain within the app's own storage.

4. Data Storage & Sync (Apple CloudKit)

If you enable sync, your data is stored in your own iCloud account via Apple CloudKit and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. As the developer, we cannot access, read, hold, or sell any of your synced content. You may manage or disable iCloud sync at any time in your system settings.

5. Crash & Diagnostic Logs

To help troubleshoot issues, the app generates runtime logs locally on your device (daily rotation, retained for up to 7 days) and does not upload them automatically. These logs contain no note content and no personally identifiable information. Logs leave your device only if you choose to email them to us. You can also clear local logs at any time within the app.

6. Payments & Purchases

In-app premium features are unlocked through Apple StoreKit 2, settled entirely between your device and Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information (such as card numbers, which are handled entirely by Apple). The premium offer consists of a one-time lifetime purchase (non-consumable) and an optional 30-day free trial before purchase (a Price Tier 0 non-consumable item, in line with the App Review Guidelines). Purchases can be restored within the app via "Restore Purchase". If you wish to request a refund, please use Apple's official refund channels (reportaproblem.apple.com) — we have no access to your payment details and cannot process or refund purchases ourselves.

7. Third-Party Tracking & Ads

This app contains no advertisements and integrates no third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your cross-app or cross-site behavior. The only third-party code bundled in the app consists of open-source libraries (see Section 8) that run entirely on your device and do not collect or transmit any data.

8. Open-Source Components

The app primarily uses Apple native frameworks and includes only a minimal set of components under permissive open-source licenses (MIT / Apache-2.0 / BSD), none of which involve data collection or transmission. These libraries are vendored locally inside the app (not loaded from remote servers) and execute entirely on your device:

  • Diagnostics — generates local crash/diagnostic reports on your device to help troubleshooting. It has no network capability; reports leave your device only when you explicitly choose to email them to us.
  • ExceptionCatcher — used internally by Diagnostics to safely catch low-level Objective-C exceptions while recording diagnostics.
  • ZIPFoundation — provides local ZIP read/write used for full-library backup/restore of your notes and mind maps. It operates on local files only.
  • KaTeX — renders LaTeX math formulas inside your notes, on-device.

The complete component list and license texts are available in the app under Settings / About - Acknowledgements & Open-Source Licenses.

9. Children's Privacy

Kaizhi Notes is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any child's personal data. If you believe your child has provided information without your knowledge, please contact us at the email above so we can address it.

10. Your Data Rights

Because your data is stored mainly on your local device and your own iCloud, you have full control: you can delete individual items or all content within the app, and you can clear local and cloud data by uninstalling the app or signing out of iCloud. We retain no server-side copies that can be linked to you.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version and date on this page and within the app. Material changes will be notified in-app.

12. Contact Us

If you have any privacy questions or requests, please email zhaoxiankai521@foxmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.
