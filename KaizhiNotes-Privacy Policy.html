Kaizhi Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026　|　Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Who We Are

This application, "Kaizhi Notes", is developed and maintained by an independent developer (referred to as "we"). If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at zhaoxiankai521@foxmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any of your personal data or note content. Kaizhi Notes is a local-first note and knowledge management tool. All of your notes, mind maps, outlines, tasks, tags, and favorites are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server controlled or operated by us.

3. Data Storage & Sync (Apple CloudKit)

If you enable sync, your data is stored in your own iCloud account via Apple CloudKit and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. As the developer, we cannot access, read, hold, or sell any of your synced content. You may manage or disable iCloud sync at any time in your system settings.

4. Crash & Diagnostic Logs

To help troubleshoot issues, the app generates runtime logs locally on your device (daily rotation, retained for up to 7 days) and does not upload them automatically. These logs contain no note content and no personally identifiable information. Logs leave your device only if you choose to email them to us. You can also clear local logs at any time within the app.

5. Payments & Purchases

In-app premium features are settled between your device and Apple through Apple StoreKit 2. We do not collect or store any payment information (such as card numbers, which are handled entirely by Apple). Subscription and purchase management is available via Apple's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

6. Third-Party Tracking & Ads

This app contains no advertisements and integrates no third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your cross-app or cross-site behavior.

7. Open-Source Components

The app primarily uses Apple native frameworks and includes only a minimal set of components under permissive open-source licenses (MIT / Apache-2.0 / BSD), none of which involve data collection. The complete component list and license texts are available in the app under Settings / About - Acknowledgements & Open-Source Licenses.

8. Children's Privacy

Kaizhi Notes is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any child's personal data. If you believe your child has provided information without your knowledge, please contact us at the email above so we can address it.

9. Your Data Rights

Because your data is stored mainly on your local device and your own iCloud, you have full control: you can delete individual items or all content within the app, and you can clear local and cloud data by uninstalling the app or signing out of iCloud. We retain no server-side copies that can be linked to you.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version and date on this page and within the app. Material changes will be notified in-app.

11. Contact Us

If you have any privacy questions or requests, please email zhaoxiankai521@foxmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.
